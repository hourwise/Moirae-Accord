# ACCORD-01 Review Response

## Response posture

The external review returned `PIVOT` in both the blind and architecture-red-team
phases. This response preserves that verdict as the review outcome while
testing the review's factual claims against the accepted ACCORD-00 evidence and
a bounded current prior-art check. The reviewer is not treated as an authority;
each material finding is classified below.

`ACCEPTED` means the finding is adopted as a project constraint or conclusion.
`PARTIALLY_ACCEPTED` means the core concern is adopted but the review's wording
or scope is narrowed. `REJECTED_WITH_EVIDENCE` means the claim is not adopted
because the cited evidence contradicts or does not support it. `DEFERRED_FOR_TEST`
means the concern is credible but must be resolved by a later pre-registered
experiment rather than asserted now.

## Net response

The review's central pivot is accepted. The project continues only as a
research/specification and verification effort. A new Accord runtime, second
policy engine, second execution-truth store, and unqualified “truth” claims are
rejected. The remaining research value, if any, must be demonstrated against a
durable-execution/provider-state baseline with an externally runnable verifier,
explicit trust roots, and experiments that expose authority composition and
effect-evidence trade-offs.

No classification above authorizes implementation. ACCORD-02 remains gated by
the binding decision document and is not started by this task.
